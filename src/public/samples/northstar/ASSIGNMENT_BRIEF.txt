NORTHSTAR LEARNING SYSTEMS - MANAGEMENT TEAM CASE

All data in this case is fictional and synthetic.

Situation
Northstar Learning Systems is a B2B SaaS company selling workforce training and simulation software. Management wants to grow from approximately $820,000 of 2026 revenue toward $1.2 million in 2027 while protecting cash and deciding whether to expand into healthcare.

Your role
You are the management team preparing a recommendation for the board. You may use AI to analyze the provided CSV files, but your team is responsible for validating the conclusions and choosing what matters.

Deliverable - 1 page or 3-minute board update
1. Is the growth plan realistic? Why or why not?
2. What are the three KPIs you would put on the CEO dashboard?
3. What is the biggest financial or operating risk?
4. Where would you invest the next $100,000?
5. What would you change in the current plan?
6. What specific data point would cause you to pivot your recommendation?

Evidence requirement
Use at least five specific data points from at least three different files.

Suggested AI prompts
- Summarize the three biggest risks in these datasets and cite the rows/fields that support each conclusion.
- Compare sales sources by conversion, pipeline quality, and likely revenue contribution.
- Identify accounts at greatest renewal risk and the operational factors associated with that risk.
- Identify customers that generate high revenue but poor contribution or high support burden.
- Pressure-test the 2027 management plan. Which assumptions appear least supported by the 2026 data?
- Recommend how to allocate the next $100,000 across product, sales, marketing, customer success, or new-market expansion.
